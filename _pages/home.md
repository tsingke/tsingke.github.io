I am an **Associate Professor** at the [School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.sdnu.edu.cn/), Shandong Normal University, and a **Master's Supervisor**. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Shandong University in 2017, and was honored as an *Outstanding Graduate of Shandong University* and *Outstanding Graduate of Shandong Province*. In 2025, I was awarded a **CSC Visiting Scholar Fellowship** by the China Scholarship Council (expected to start in 2027).

My research focuses on **swarm intelligence**, **evolutionary computation**, and **parallel intelligent computing**, with an emphasis on fundamental theory, optimization algorithms, and engineering applications for high-dimensional, dynamic, noisy, and expensive black-box optimization problems.

## Research Interests

- Swarm Intelligence
- Evolutionary Computation
- Learning-assisted / Deep-learning-assisted Optimization
- Large-scale Optimization
- GPU/CUDA Parallel Intelligent Computing
- Bioinformatics and Engineering Optimization

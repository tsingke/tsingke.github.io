The **Intelligent Computing Group** (山师大智能计算研究课题组) at Shandong Normal University focuses on the frontier of intelligent optimization and computational intelligence, including swarm intelligence, evolutionary computation, learning-assisted optimization, and GPU-parallel intelligent computing.

We adhere to the educational philosophy of **"tailoring teaching to each student and unifying knowledge with practice"** (因材施教，知行合一), emphasizing the deep integration of theory and practice, and inspiring students to pursue **"self-cultivation through inquiry and innovation"** (格物致知，守正创新).

We warmly welcome outstanding students to join us. Contact: **tsingke@sdnu.edu.cn**.

I have supervised more than 20 master's students. My students have won the National Graduate Scholarship and Excellent Master's Thesis awards, and have published dozens of papers in top-tier SCI journals (JCR Q1/Q2).

**Recruitment.** I recruit **3-4 master's students per year** in:
- Computer Science and Technology (academic track)
- Computer Technology (professional track)

Students with a solid foundation in computer science, artificial intelligence, mathematics, or bioinformatics — and with strong motivation for academic research or engineering development — are warmly welcome to join the Intelligent Computing Group.
